Our Story

Why four floors?
Because one wasn't enough.

4Stories started with a simple idea: what if every floor told a different story? In the middle of Thamel's beautiful chaos, we carved out a space where mornings feel slow, afternoons stretch, and nights come alive.

From the ground-floor coffee bar where espresso meets conversation, to the rooftop where cocktails meet the Kathmandu skyline — every level was designed to make you forget whatever's waiting outside.

We're not just a restaurant. We're four worlds under one roof. Come find yours.
